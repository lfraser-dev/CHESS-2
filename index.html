What happened

    Inkmate

    Chess on a bigger board, scribbled in a notebook. Earn coins, rip open card packs, and bend the rules: landmines, curses, wrap-around edges, mind control, dragons, and the odd F-15.

    • Normal chess rules on a 10×10 board, plus two Archers each.
    • Each turn, move a piece or play a card from your hand.
    • You get 1 coin a turn and more for captures. Packs cost 5–7, and buying one doesn't use your turn.
    • Four collab sets: Iron Crusade, Dust & Daring, Monster Mayhem and Plunder. Recycle cards you don't want.
    • Checkmate the enemy king to win.
