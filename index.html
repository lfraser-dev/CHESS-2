What happened

    Chess 2

    the sequel nobody asked for

    Chess, but completely unhinged. Pomegranates, markers, gems, wedding rings, dance parties, pieces walking off the board, and somebody might drop a nuke.

    • Checkmate the enemy king. That's the only rule that stays put.
    • Each turn, move a piece or play a card. Coins buy packs of 127 ridiculous cards.
    • Random things keep turning up on the board: a pomegranate, a marker, gems, a wedding ring, a disco ball, a birthday cake. Walk a piece onto one and see what happens.
    • Armies start shuffled, with a few weird units mixed in. Capturing can make a piece mutate.
    • Every piece has special moves: tap a piece to see them. The Brainrot meter fills every turn and unlocks crazier ones, act by act, up to the Finale.
