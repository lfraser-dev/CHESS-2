What happened

    Inkmate

    Chess on a bigger board, scribbled in a notebook. Earn coins, rip open card packs, and bend the rules: landmines, curses, wrap-around edges, mind control, dragons, and the odd F-15.

    • A normal chess army each (plus two Archers), on a 15×15 board. Scroll or pinch to zoom in, drag to look around.
    • Each turn, move a piece or play a card from your hand.
    • You get 1 coin a turn and more for captures. A pack costs 5 and opening one doesn't use your turn.
    • 108 cards in one pack: pagodas, grandmas, pigeons, curses, landmines… and the NUKE. Recycle the ones you don't want.
    • Checkmate the enemy king to win.
