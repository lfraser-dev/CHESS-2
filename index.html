    Inkmate

    Chess on a bigger board, scribbled in a notebook. Win coins, rip open card packs, and drop dragons, tanks and the odd F-15 into the game.

    • Normal chess rules on a 10×10 board, plus two Archers each.
    • Each turn, move a piece or play a card from your hand.
    • You get 1 coin a turn and more for captures. A pack costs 5 and doesn't use your turn.
    • Checkmate the enemy king to win.
